LAST TRICK STANDING

How to play

Win at least one trick to survive. Be the last player remaining.

1. The rounds

Round 1 starts with 7 cards per player. Each new round deals one fewer card: 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, then 1.

2. Trump

In Round 1, the top card of the remaining deck is turned over. Its suit becomes trump.

From Round 2 onward, the player who won the most tricks in the previous round chooses trump after seeing their new hand.

3. Playing a trick

The player to the dealer's right leads the first card. After that, the winner of each trick leads the next one.

  • You must follow the suit that was led when you can.
  • If you cannot follow suit, you may play any card.
  • Any trump beats every non-trump card.
  • If several trumps are played, the highest trump wins.
  • If no trump is played, the highest card of the led suit wins.

4. Surviving a round

Every trick you win counts as 1. Win at least one trick to stay in the game.

Players who win no tricks are eliminated — except in Round 1, when they receive their one Doggie Life and may continue into Round 2.

5. Who chooses next trump?

The player with the most tricks wins the round and chooses trump for the next round.

If players tie for the most tricks, they cut cards. Highest card wins the cut. Equal ranks cut again.

6. Turn timer

You have 30 seconds to play. If time runs out, the game automatically plays a legal card from your hand.

7. Winning the game

The last player remaining wins. The one-card final round is pot luck: everyone has only one card, and the normal follow-suit and trump rules still decide the winner.

Strategy tip Your first priority is one safe trick. After that, extra tricks can earn you control of next round's trump.
